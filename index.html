Glentworth Group operates across two core business streams, Land Development and Building. A boutique practice, we take on a selective number of projects and apply the same rigorous standard to each.

Land Development
Land Development

From identifying the right opportunity through to settlement, Glentworth Group manages the full land development lifecycle. We bring together the right people, capital and expertise to deliver residential development projects across Western Australia.

Land Opportunity Identification

We actively source and assess residential land opportunities across the Perth metro area, applying rigorous commercial analysis to identify sites with genuine development potential.

Investor Syndication

Where appropriate, we bring together aligned investors to participate in development opportunities, managing the relationship, structure and governance with transparency and professionalism.

Negotiation & Acquisition

We negotiate directly with vendors and agents to secure sites on the best available terms, drawing on deep commercial experience to structure offers that work for all parties.

Subdivision & Development Approvals

We manage the full approvals process, including subdivision applications, development applications and engagement with local government, to deliver a clear and compliant pathway to construction.

Construction & Development Delivery

With our own registered building capability, we deliver the construction phase of development projects directly, maintaining quality, programme and cost control from slab to practical completion.

Sales & Settlement

We manage the sales process through to settlement, working with agents, solicitors and buyers to deliver a smooth and professional conclusion to each development project.

Building
Building
Building

As a Registered Building Practitioner in Western Australia, Glentworth Group provides a full range of building services to private clients, developers and investors. We are selective about the projects we accept and personally oversee every engagement.

Traditional Building Contractor

We act as head contractor for new residential builds, managing all trades, materials, inspections and programme delivery under a formal building contract. Our clients deal directly with the principal, not a project manager.

Modern Methods of Construction

We actively pursue and deliver projects using modern construction methodologies, including modular and prefabricated building systems. These approaches offer faster delivery, reduced waste and greater cost predictability for residential development.

Construction Management

For clients who require independent oversight without engaging a head contractor, we provide professional construction management services, covering programme, cost, quality and subcontractor coordination from award to handover.

Distressed Projects & Crisis Management

When a build has gone wrong, whether through builder insolvency, dispute, abandonment or defective workmanship, we step in. Our director's background as an appointed expert witness in construction disputes gives us a rare ability to assess the situation clearly, advise on the options and execute a decisive recovery.
